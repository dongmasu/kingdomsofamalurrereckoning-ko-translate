# Mitharan Versus Mitharu: D-19670 to D-19671

The official Korean manual establishes the **deity** `Mitharu =
미사루`; it does not establish `Mitharan = 미사루식`. Cross-checking
earlier approved D-1096 and D-19551, existing base equipment, and
D-194's explicit uncertainty for `Mitharans` corrected the overly
broad deity-derived wording in D-19666, D-19668 and D-19669.
English separates the ethnic/cultural adjective `Mitharan` from
the god's name `Mitharu`. Japanese and Chinese corroborate the
distinction contextually but do not settle a universal Korean
ethnonym; use the already established `미타란` form for these
specific four equipment names and the Old Mitharan language.
No PAKs were consulted.
