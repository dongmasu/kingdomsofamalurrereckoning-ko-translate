# Five Asset Revalidation: Aster, Ratofer, Arne, Anira, and Sorrows Contexts

- Date: 2026-11-11.
- Scope: 109 unique `default` IDs across five previously unrecorded assets.
- English anchors meaning; Japanese is the primary style/register reference;
  Chinese supports the comparison.
- Eleven correction candidates and ten evidence-limited holds were recorded.
  The Korean translation CSV was not changed.

## Grouping Evidence and Limits

All five assets lack an extracted `QUEST_ID` and `NPC_` symbol. The two
quest-text assets `734522.quest_bxml` and `717566.quest_bxml` have no
`dialog_line_*` handlers. `557394.quest_bxml` has 14 handlers with an
`SQ30_FIN` prefix; `1825868.quest_bxml` has seven with an `SQ154` prefix;
`1747110.quest_bxml` has 112 with an `FP_NQD_Citizens` prefix. These are
asset-level clues only. They do not map a localization key to a speaker,
dialogue handler, branch, or playback position. Speaker attribution and
dialogue order remain unknown.

The matched rows include quest objectives, journal/status entries, names and
item labels, and dialogue-like text. The resource associations do not imply
that all rows in a file belong to one conversation or one character.

four locales; no embedded line breaks or runtime tokens occur in this batch.

or dialogue sequence was inferred.
