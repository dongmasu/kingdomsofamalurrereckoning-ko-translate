## Decisions

- ID 19302 remains uncertain: the 23-line devotional schedule and its
  structure are preserved, but the religious register, named rites, and
  source typo `form` require broader terminology/context review.
- IDs 19308 and 19311 remain uncertain: the comic `BOOM` and insulting letter
  are preserved, but the sabotage device and malformed English phrase
  `tha tlittle` do not establish a definitive Korean wording.
- IDs 19314 and 19318 remain uncertain: the familiar `my child` address and
  `Hireling` label are plausible, but speaker register and whether the role is
  an apprentice, employee, or hired fighter remain open.
- IDs 19315-19316, 19324, and 19328-19330 remain uncertain: the dialogue
  meanings are broadly retained, but the canonical forms of Kandrian,
  Idylla, Ametair, Buru, Deirdre Gwint, Herc, and Tywili and Ametair's
  personification are not settled.
- IDs 19317 and 19320 remain uncertain: the ingredient/item labels and
  parenthetical retention convention need terminology alignment; the
  quantity `3` is preserved.
- ID 19325 is accepted: change `너 때문에 그가 하지 않을 행동이 생기고,
  태어나지 못할 아이들도 생겼어...` to `너 때문에 그가 하지 않게 된 일도,
  태어나지 못할 아이들도 있어...`. English describes deeds he will not do
  and children who will not be born; the prior Korean awkwardly says those
  actions “arise.” This matches the supported causal sense and the approved
  wording at ID 1570 without adding context.
- ID 19331 is accepted: change `아니라는 이제 없지만` to
  `아니라(Anira)는 이제 없지만`. English names Anira and Japanese says she
  is no longer here; the current Korean is misread as the conjunction
  `아니라`. Preserve “gone” without asserting death, which only Chinese
  makes explicit.
- ID 19333 remains uncertain: the unusual source description of Scholia
  Arcana as a `journal and lecture society` is not resolved by the differing
  institutional readings in Japanese and Chinese.
- ID 19334 is `not an issue`: Korean preserves the speaker's contempt for
  his deserters and the hope that the Tuatha will beat them.
- ID 19338 remains uncertain: `원소 마법을 다루는` is one plausible reading
  of `elemental`, but Japanese and Chinese support other descriptions of
  Conni.
- ID 19339 remains uncertain: the English source itself includes
  `(Malvinas)`; whether to retain that parenthetical in this isolated UI label
  is not established.
- ID 19341 remains uncertain: `the House` may be the House of Sorrows, but
  the English line alone does not identify it; avoid confirming `비탄의 집`
  in this occurrence.
- ID 19343 is `not an issue`: the Korean retains the speaker's revenge,
  lack of relief, and increased longing; it does not need to repeat “death”
  to preserve the emotional consequence.
- ID 19345 is `not an issue`: `협상해야 해` is supported by the English
  idiom `treat with` in context, and Korean retains the caution and maiden Fae.
- ID 19346 is `not an issue`: `운명 없는 자` clearly preserves the
  established player-title meaning of `Fateless One`.
- ID 19347 remains uncertain: `Plagueshield Tincture` is retained
  provisionally, but the project's canonical item name and `tincture`
  terminology remain open.
- ID 19348 remains uncertain: Korean `영주` follows English `Lord` and
  Chinese, while Japanese says “king”; the rank and canonical place/name form
  need context.
