# Five Asset Revalidations: Riagan, Thresh, Tala-Rane, Motus, and King's Thimble Contexts

- Date: 2026-11-11.
- Scope: 158 unique `default` IDs across five previously unrecorded assets.
- English anchors meaning; Japanese is the primary style/register reference;
  Chinese supports the comparison.
- Seven correction candidates and three evidence-limited holds were recorded.
  The Korean translation CSV was not changed.

## Grouping Evidence and Limits

None of these assets contains an explicit `QUEST_ID` or `NPC_` symbol.
`533430.quest_bxml` has eight handlers with an `SQ22` prefix;
`1700657.quest_bxml` has 13 handlers with an `NQD_Region_TalaRane` prefix;
and `1704224.quest_bxml` has 11 handlers with an `SQ153` prefix. The other
two assets have no `dialog_line_*` handlers. These asset and handler clues
support topic-level grouping only; they do not map a localization key to an
individual speaker, handler, branch, or playback position. Speaker
attribution and dialogue order remain unknown.

The matched rows include quest objectives and journal entries, dialogue-like
text, names, item labels, and other UI text. The reports group related text
by quest or topic where the source supports it, without treating all rows in
an asset as one conversation.
