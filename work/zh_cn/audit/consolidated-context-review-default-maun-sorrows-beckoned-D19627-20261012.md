# Consolidated Context Review: Maun, Sorrows, and Beckoned (D-19627)

- Date: 2026-10-12.
- Scope: seven open findings D-1080-D-1082, D-1084, and D-1086-D-1088;
  default IDs 4536, 4537, 4539, 4546, and 4553-4555.
- English was the meaning anchor; Japanese and Chinese were cross-checked.
  Linked source keys, accepted terminology decisions, and relevant sections
  of the official Korean manual were reviewed. No PAK material was used.

## Decisions

| Decision / ID | Disposition | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-1080 / 4536 | accepted | English IDs 11800 and 12695 establish Maun as a thief who fabricated his hoard to spite his enemies. Japanese transliterates `Cointaker`; Chinese translates the coin reference. The existing `동전 수집가` misleadingly suggests a hobby, so reuse the exact repeated-name form at ID 2281, `마운 코인트레이커(Maun Cointaker)`, without deciding whether `Cointaker` is a surname or epithet. |
| D-1081 / 4537 | accepted | English ID 64074 defines the Sorrows as a magical-energy force, and accepted D-18322 renders that same force `비탄의 힘`. Japanese personifies sorrow as the source of the information, while Chinese uses a sorrow-spirit expression. Align this occurrence to the established force term; do not confuse it with the separate House of Sorrows faction listed in the manual. |
| D-1082 / 4539 | not an issue | English identifies Motus Mining's home office and its choice to station itself with the Bursar's; Japanese says headquarters near the accounting institute, and Chinese says the financial department. Korean preserves both the company headquarters and nearby finance office without changing the business reason. |
| D-1084 / 4546 | accepted | English says the group has entered times beyond what the speaker's cards have seen; Chinese explicitly says outside the cards' vision, and Japanese says the future read by the cards has passed. Replace the less exact `내 카드가 보여 준 시절은 지났군` with `내 카드가 본 적 없는 시대에 들어섰군`. The manual's page 5 confirms that Fate and Fate Twists are represented by tarot cards, supporting the card-reading context. |
| D-1086 / 4553 | not an issue | English and Chinese retain an unspecified `it`, while Japanese omits the subject. Korean preserves that deliberate omission and the stated chronology before Ashmoor, Eventide, the House of Vengeance, and Winter Court; no unsupported antecedent is added. |
| D-1087 / 4554 | not an issue | English `combination`, Japanese `組み合わせ`, and Chinese `密码` support a lock/code combination. Korean `암호 조합` preserves that broad sense without inventing the lock or container. |
| D-1088 / 4555 | accepted | English addresses the player by the established title `Beckoned`; reuse D-692/D-3115's accepted `부름받은 자` rather than the over-specific `초대받은 자`. The thanks and impending undertaking remain intact. |
