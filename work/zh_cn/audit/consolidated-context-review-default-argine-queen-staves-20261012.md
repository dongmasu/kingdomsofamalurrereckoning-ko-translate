## Finding

The English rows repeat the exact label `Argine, Queen of Staves`; Japanese
consistently identifies the same named queen and staff wording, and Chinese
uses the corresponding staff-queen label. These repetitions confirm the
shared surface referent and title, but none establishes whether `Queen of
Staves` is a tarot suit/card designation, a formal character title, or both.
The Korean rows preserve the named figure/title relation but vary in the
rendering of `Staves` and in whether the name is parenthesized.

D-10940 at ID 33892 only removed the unsupported/redundant `홀` from one
Korean line and used ID 31796 as a local wording precedent. It did not settle
the tarot/card versus character-title question or authorize corpus-wide
normalization of the name, suit, or parenthetical policy. D-10306 and the
other listed source decisions explicitly retain those questions.

Disposition: **retain all sixteen holds as uncertain**. Do not apply the
candidate five-cell normalization. In particular, repeated identical
English labels are not evidence that a card interpretation or a single
canonical Korean spelling has been established.
