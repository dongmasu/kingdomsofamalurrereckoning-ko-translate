# Early Titles, Bow, and Dolve Arne Review (D-19593)

- Date: 2026-10-12. Grouped existing D-194 through D-209 holds
  by title, named place, narrative, and gameplay sense. Exact English,
  Japanese and Chinese keys were compared. The official Korean manual's
  PDF pages 1, 4 and 9 give world/region, skill and faction context, but do
  not name Mitharans, Scions or Dolve Arne; absence is not a reason to
  hold otherwise source-faithful Korean. No PAK material was used.
