# Hierophant Shrine Context Review (D-19572)

- Date: 2026-10-12.
- Scope: `default` ID 189, previously held as D-053.
- Disposition: `not an issue`.
- Evidence: English is the meaning anchor; Japanese informs voice and
  terminology; Chinese, German, Spanish, French, and Italian are
  cross-checks. The official Korean manual was consulted for Travelers and
  faction context. No PAK or PAK-derived evidence was used.

## Finding

English ID 189 is the short label `Hierophant Shrine`. Its function and
ownership are explicit in nearby English context: ID 81 says the shrine
instructed a Traveler to prove their worth; ID 1758 identifies the
Hierophant as ruler of the Travelers; ID 4386 says people pray at the
Hierophant's shrines for visions; and ID 11140 calls the shrine the place
to pray to the anonymous Traveler leader. The same label recurs at ID
12337.

Japanese ID 189 says `"導き手"の祭壇` (“Guide's altar”); Chinese says
`大祭司神殿` (“High Priest's temple”). German, Spanish, French, and Italian
use cognates of Hierophant and describe its shrine/altar. These differences
do not change the place's sacred function or its relation to the Travelers'
leader. Korean `대사제의 성소` preserves both the title's high-priest
sense and the sacred place. The Korean term need not be forced to a
transliteration merely because some European locales retain a cognate.

The official Korean manual's printed page 14 lists Travelers among the
game's factions; printed pages 17-18 introduce factions broadly. It does
not name or define the Hierophant. The manual's general setting context
does not contradict the row-level translation.

Close D-053 as `not an issue`. This is a row-level finding and does not
authorize corpus-wide replacement of `대사제`, `계시자`, or
`히에로판트`.
