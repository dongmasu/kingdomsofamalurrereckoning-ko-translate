# Consolidated Context Review: Crilgarin, Knave of Coins

- Review date: 2026-10-11.
- Scope: twenty previously uncertain Korean keys for the recurring
  `Crilgarin, Knave of Coins` title, including its explicit explanation at
  ID 63397.
- Result: sixteen accepted corrections and four `not an issue`; one exact
  duplicate row, ID 16890, was updated but not recounted.

## Evidence

English repeats the exact title `Crilgarin, Knave of Coins` at IDs 12388,
12443, 14181, 14630, 14680, 16110, 16849, 16890, 18512, 18580, 21264,
23460, 26496, 28374, 29873, 32244, 38270, 40661, 49361, 51009, 53769,
55915, 58850, 60793, 62179, and 64624. English ID 63397 explicitly calls
`Knave of Coins` Crilgarin's title and says it grants her responsibility
for the camp and its Travelers. Japanese shifts between “servant” and
“master” of gold coins, while Chinese uses a coin-associated rogue; English
controls the title meaning.

Accepted decisions D-4435 and D-4450 already apply `동전의 잭` to this exact
English label at IDs 14630 and 14680. D-17986 applies the same form at
62179. Those precedents support a consistent local rendering without
claiming that every use of `Knave` elsewhere is a card rank.
