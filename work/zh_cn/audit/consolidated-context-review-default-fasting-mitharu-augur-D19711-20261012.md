  prophet, and the accepted Augur identity identify a person,

  16507-16508, 16516, 16530. The lowercase `fastings`

  the in-game description, rather than guessing at a typo.

  Mithros, Hugues' evasiveness, Fist-weapon tutorial, and
  Fae Cairns ballad instruction preserve English meanings.
