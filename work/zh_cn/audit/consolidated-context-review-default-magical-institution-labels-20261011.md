# Consolidated Context Review: Magical Institution Labels

- Date: 2026-10-11.
- Scope: previously uncertain standalone labels for `Court of Enchantments`,
  `Scholia Arcana`, and `The Font`.
- Method: compare English, Japanese, Chinese, the Japanese-derived Korean
  draft, active Korean, prior decisions, and repeated source context by ID.
  English controls meaning; Japanese informs voice/register; Chinese is
  supporting evidence.
- Result: D-18237 and D-18238 reclassify seven existing uncertain keys as
  `not an issue`. D-18239 rechecks three `The Font` keys and retains their
  existing uncertainty. No CSV cells changed.

## D-18237: Court of Enchantments (16946)

ID 16946 is the standalone English label `Court of Enchantments`. Japanese
`祝福の宮殿` describes a palace of blessings, while Chinese `魔法廷` supports
the magic-court sense; active Korean and the Japanese-derived draft both use
`마법의 궁정`. The English entity is the same House of Ballads court reviewed
at D-18235, and accepted D-1815 establishes `마법의 궁정` as its Korean form.
The Japanese variation does not identify a Korean row-level error. Reclassify
ID 16946 from uncertain to `not an issue`; no edit.

## D-18238: Scholia Arcana (Six Standalone Labels)

IDs 16312, 25400, 29179, 30269, 31625, and 31862 each contain only the
English label `Scholia Arcana`. Japanese uses `アルカナ大学` and Chinese
`奥术学院`, both identifying an arcane academic institution. All six active
Korean cells already use the accepted proper-name form `스콜리아 아르카나`;
some retain an English parenthetical and others do not.

Accepted D-1397, D-3146, D-6297, D-6368, D-9985, and D-18206 establish the
Korean name. D-18207 also retained already-canonical standalone labels
without imposing a parenthetical-English rule. The six current labels
therefore preserve the named institution and need no row-local correction.
Reclassify these six uncertain keys as `not an issue`; no edits.

## D-18239: The Font (1331, 12405, 14580)

All three rows are the exact English label `The Font`; Japanese identifies
`イーサの泉` (Ysa's spring), Chinese uses `圣泉` (sacred spring), and the draft
and active Korean both use `샘(Font)`. The evidence supports a specific
magical water source, but it does not settle the canonical Korean display
form or establish that the bare parenthetical `Font` is interchangeable
with the previously accepted `샘(The Font)` at ID 10833 (D-3127). That
row-specific approval is not generalized here. Retain the three existing
uncertain dispositions and do not edit.
