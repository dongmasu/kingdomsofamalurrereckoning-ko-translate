## Decisions

- IDs 20101 and 20112 are `not an issue`: `복구 작업` preserves the broad
  restoration sense without choosing between repair and reconstruction.
- ID 20102 is accepted: revise the clause about gamblers to make clear that
  they profit from the fights rather than receive money directly from the
  fighters.
- IDs 20103, 20104, 20110, 20115, and 20117-20118 remain uncertain:
  Telling, murghan, Evenroot, Kollossae, Firstsworn, and The Crymbil are
  retained, but canonical lore terms and names need alignment.
- ID 20106 is accepted: change `안 돼!` to `아니!`. Japanese and Chinese
  support a direct “No,” and the following line says the addressee has a duty
  to listen.
- ID 20107 is accepted: change `전쟁 영웅에게 누명을 씌우고` to
  `전쟁 영웅을 망신 주고`. English, Japanese, and Chinese say disgrace/
  humiliate/shame, not falsely accuse.
- ID 20113 is accepted: change `그래도 모두 알려야 해` to
  `그래도 그리안 셰인에게 이 일은 알려야 해`, preserving the specific
  matter that Grian must hear about without implying “tell everything.”
- ID 20121 is accepted: change `행진해 오기` to `걸어오기`; this avoids
  an unsupported military/ceremonial sense for refugees arriving with their
  belongings.
- IDs 20122, 20126-20128, 20130, and 20135-20136 remain uncertain: Scion,
  Reed House, Castellan, Fateweaving, Travelers, the name-change placeholder,
  and Warsworn terminology need context; the speaker's hesitation remains.
- ID 20133 is `not an issue`: Korean preserves the speaker's grief-driven
  theatricality and the wife's disappearance.
- ID 20141 is accepted: change `피의 도제` to `피의 지도자` for `Blood
  Doge`. The current Korean means an apprentice; Japanese says “blood
  leader” and Chinese says “blood governor.”
