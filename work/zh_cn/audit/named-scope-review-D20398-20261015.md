# D-20398: Macit and Magessa Name Consistency

Date: 2026-10-15

## Scope and Decisions

Reviewed all six indexed occurrences for Macit, Senecer Macit, Magessa, and
Magessa Ohr in `default` IDs 8849, 18927, 19472, 28023, 34173, and 50002.
English identifies the people and full names. Japanese and Chinese support
the same identities and name readings; the official Korean manual extraction
has no entry for either NPC.

Accepted four `Texto` corrections: annotate Senecer Macit's full name at
8849, normalize the Macit component at 28023 to the already established
`마싯`, annotate the full name Magessa Ohr at 34173, and add
`마게사(Magessa)` at the clear personal-name reference in 50002. The
correctly selected subject particle at 50002 is preserved. The standalone
labels at 18927 and 19472 already use their confirmed bilingual forms and
are `not-an-issue`.

The two compound people remain separate glossary entries alongside their
component names. This six-row pass does not disposition other M-N proposal
rows or all occurrences in other resources. The generated M-N proposal
output is still a draft and must not be treated as independently reviewed.
No prior semantic finding was reopened or globally recounted.
