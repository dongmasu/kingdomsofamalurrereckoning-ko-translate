- Decision date: 2026-10-09.

- Twenty-three findings received dispositions: four accepted, eleven `not an

## Decisions

- ID 13350 is `not an issue`: `님프` is a clear Korean rendering of `Nymph`;
  the objective's meaning is preserved.
- ID 13351 remains uncertain: Lunala, the Watch office, and Eriad Talibor
  need canonical Korean name/title forms.
- ID 13352 remains uncertain: the isolated `Font` label may be a named
  spring/location, and Njordir's established spelling needs confirmation.
- ID 13353 is `not an issue`: Korean follows English and Chinese's direct
  wish to return to Mel Senshir; Japanese differs.
- ID 13355 remains uncertain: `Tenders` and their role in Winter Fae
  decomposition require lore context.
- ID 13358 remains uncertain: the devotional `votives` are not identified
  consistently as candles, ornaments, or wishing stones.
- ID 13359 is `not an issue`: the example/comparison between Tirin's Rest and
  Culn is supported by Japanese and Chinese; Korean preserves both names.
- ID 13362 is accepted: replace `스승을 잃은 견습생` with `스승 없는 견습생`.
  English `master-less` does not say that the apprentice's master died;
  Japanese adds that implication, while English does not. The separate Tallow
  and Doorsill terminology questions remain open for corpus review.
- ID 13365 is `not an issue`: `윈더미어의 시녀` uses the established Korean
  title and preserves the question.
- ID 13370 is accepted: replace the malformed stat ordering with
  `+7 장궁 물리 피해` and `+30% 장궁 피해`. Both longbow bonuses and their
  values are explicit in English; the corrected notation follows existing
  Korean longbow-stat patterns and retains the line break.
- ID 13375 is `not an issue`: Korean preserves the Winter Fae identity, the
  Ettin threat, and the instruction to rescue Taibreah whether or not this is
  part of the trial.
- ID 13376 is `not an issue`: Korean retains the speaker's surprise at the
  scout/thief identity and suspicion that the friendship may have been false.
- ID 13377 remains uncertain: the Testament may be a named faction, though
  the mercenary motive and two-person job are preserved.
- ID 13380 is `not an issue`: `대가를 치르게 될 거야` keeps the broad threat
  without narrowing `pay` to money.
- ID 13381 is accepted: replace `아이를 많이 낳아 키우고` with
  `아이들을 많이 키우고`. English, Japanese, and Chinese say “raise many
  children”; they do not state that the speaker will give birth to them.
- ID 13382 remains uncertain: Kolmir, ettin, war-priest, and Tala-Rane forms
  need terminology confirmation.
- ID 13385 remains uncertain: the causal relationship between the Hyperian's
  raising, expanded power, and seeing the gods' challenge remains unclear.
- ID 13389 is accepted: replace the literal `놈의 피를 보아라!` with
  `놈을 죽여!`. Japanese explicitly says “kill him,” and English/Chinese
  support the threat to spill the target's blood; the current Korean reads
  unnaturally as an instruction to look at blood.
- ID 13391 is `not an issue`: Korean uses the established `패란즈` form and
  preserves the remaining underground creatures without inventing their
  identity.
- ID 13395 is `not an issue`: the letter is correctly described as
  advertising a great hunt across Amalur.
- ID 13396 is `not an issue`: Korean preserves the need to keep the door open,
  the ally's likely death, and the forced choice.
- ID 13398 is `not an issue`: `사기를 북돋우다` is a natural rendering of
  raising the collective spirit/morale.
- ID 13399 remains uncertain: Hugues appears as both `휴그` and `휴즈` in the
  active corpus; this row should wait for a corpus-wide spelling decision.
