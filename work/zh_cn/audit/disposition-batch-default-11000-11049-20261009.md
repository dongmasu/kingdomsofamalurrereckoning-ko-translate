## Decisions

- ID 11003 remains uncertain: the active corpus uses several forms for
  Kollossae, and the official manual was not searchable here. Do not choose a
  canonical ethnonym from transliteration alone.
- ID 11005 remains uncertain: English capitalizes `Codex`, while Japanese
  indicates a book/document and Chinese a code/canon. The row alone does not
  establish the object's category.
- ID 11013 remains uncertain: `마녀 기사 아니라(Anira)` matches the
  provisional form at 10978, but title spacing and the character's canonical
  Korean name remain unresolved.
- ID 11016 remains uncertain: `Revival` may be the camp's proper name or a
  generic description; the Chinese adds “Camp,” but no canonical form is
  established.
- ID 11019 is `not an issue`: the seventh memoir chapter, treasure clue, and
  final-portion location are retained. The English's “final portion” remains
  broad rather than being narrowed to a chapter.
- ID 11022 remains uncertain: the sources identify a reconstructed crown
  called the Amrynthian Band, but active ID 500 calls the related name
  `아므린티안 반지`; artifact naming and the crown/band relationship need
  corpus-wide resolution.
- ID 11024 is `not an issue`: the broken Arrivus Engine is repaired through
  effort and luck. The Chinese “disassembler” reading is contradicted by
  English and Japanese.
- ID 11027 is accepted: change `이브닝타이드(Eventide)` to
  `이븐타이드(Eventide)`, matching the repeated active form at IDs 641, 819,
  1200, and 1562.
- ID 11028 is `not an issue`: Korean makes the malformed English concession
  readable while retaining the years of work, ruined fortune, and successful
  lightning-forged blade; Japanese supports the same progression.
- ID 11033 remains uncertain: `the Sorrows` may denote the House/faction,
  its members, or another group; English pronouns do not settle who must be
  awakened. Preserve the current reading pending lore context.
- ID 11034 remains uncertain: `House` may mean a named Fae institution or a
  physical residence. Korean preserves both the empty House and departed
  occupants without resolving the category.
- ID 11040 remains uncertain: the weapon, Niskaru challenge, burial, Ljosalfar
  affiliation, Dene Ealan, and Arcane Age are retained, but the proper-name
  forms need integrated terminology review.
- ID 11041 remains uncertain: `Truesworn` has competing active Korean forms
  and may be a rank, faction, or epithet. Do not normalize this row alone.
- ID 11042 remains uncertain: `픽(Pick)` preserves the source term and
  confiscation/sending sequence, but the item type is not identified.
- ID 11045 is accepted: change `놈 사회` to `노움 사회`; English and Chinese
  clearly mean gnomish society, and the active Korean consistently uses
  `노움` for gnomes.
- ID 11048 remains uncertain: “humble agent of change” may be an idiom or
  deliberate title. Japanese, English, and Chinese frame the speaker
  differently; no fixed political or religious sense is established.
- ID 11049 is accepted: change `열다섯, 아직 풋내기였을 때였지` to
  `열다섯 살의 어린 나이였을 때였지`. English `tender` and Japanese's
  fifteen-year-old-girl phrasing describe youth; `풋내기` adds an unsupported
  implication of inexperience.
