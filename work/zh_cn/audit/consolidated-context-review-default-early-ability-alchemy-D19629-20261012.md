| D-1129 / 4680 | accepted | English `act of conviction` calls for an act expressing conviction/resolution, while Japanese `説得` supports persuasion and Chinese offers a different trial/judgment reading. Replace the awkward calque `확신의 행동` with the natural, source-broad `결의를 보여 주는 행동`; retain that it is not a traditional ritual. |
| D-1130 / 4681 | accepted | English `Commentaries on the Practice of Alchemy`, Japanese `錬金術に関する注釈`, and Chinese `对炼金术实践的评论` all describe commentary on alchemical practice. `실습` suggests a training exercise or workbook; `연금술 실천 해설서` preserves the broader title. |
| D-1131 / 4682 | not an issue | English/Japanese/Chinese state that whatever manifests is private to each applicant and is not discussed by tradition. Korean `지원자` and the privacy rule preserve this without inventing the nature of the event. |
| D-1132 / 4690 | not an issue | English's final fragment leaves the exact antecedent implicit; Korean attaches invisibility to the Fae knowledge embedded in the stone, a reading supported by the preceding sentence and Chinese. No source-backed alternative requires changing the line. |
| D-1135 / 4698 | not an issue | The standalone Hierophant label uses the same `대사제` established at ID 4462 and in D-1125; no row-local inconsistency remains. |
| D-1136 / 4699 | not an issue | English `Contact`, Japanese `連絡してきた相手`, and Chinese `联系人` identify a person serving as the player's contact. Korean `연락책 돕기` is a natural quest objective. |
