# Consolidated Context Review: Early Lore and Names (D-19630)

- Date: 2026-10-12.
- Scope: seven open findings D-1137, D-1141, D-1143, D-1146,
  D-1150, and D-1153-D-1154; default IDs 4711, 4722, 4727, 4746,
  4753, 4768, and 4769.
- English was checked as the meaning anchor, with matching Japanese and

  corpus form were checked.
- No PAK or PAK-derived material was consulted.

## Decisions

| Decision / ID | Disposition | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-1137 / 4711 | not an issue | English `Crows' Handicap`, Japanese `クロウズのハンデ`, and Chinese `乌鸦的障碍` all leave the exact mechanic unspecified. Korean `크로우즈(The Crows)의 핸디캡` preserves that broad label without guessing a betting rule or arena modifier. |
| D-1141 / 4722 | not an issue | The English verse itself has malformed punctuation/rhyme and no closing quotation mark; Japanese and Chinese reorganize the verse differently. Korean preserves the reassurance that Nix has no faults to mend and does not invent a repair to the source's rhyme or quotation. |
| D-1143 / 4727 | not an issue | English capitalizes `The Understyle` and Japanese transliterates it; Chinese gives a literal lower-garden reading. Korean `언더스타일(The Understyle)` preserves the named reading and remains traceable without imposing the Chinese location interpretation. |
| D-1146 / 4746 | not an issue | English `The Inferno` and Japanese `炎獄` permit a named realm; Chinese uses a generic hell reading. Korean `불지옥` conveys the source's infernal sense without inventing a more specific entity or place type. |
| D-1150 / 4753 | accepted | The official manual context and accepted D-19602 / ID 2348 establish the House of Sorrows as `비탄의 집`. Replace the inconsistent `슬픔의 집`; Japanese `我が種族` and Chinese `我的族人` also support Korean `내 종족` for `my kind`. |
| D-1153 / 4768 | not an issue | All locales retain the sleeping dead, mourning hymn, and earth-embrace/embalming image. Korean preserves that personification and the four-line structure; the awkward source image does not establish a semantic mistranslation. |
| D-1154 / 4769 | accepted | D-19602 / ID 2352 accepted `크리살라이 씨앗(chrysalae)` for this same lore term after English/Japanese/Chinese context established the seed sense. Align both repetitions in this row to that approved Korean form. |
