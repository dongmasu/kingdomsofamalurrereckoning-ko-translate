## Decisions

  preserved, but it is unclear whether Rat Queen is a creature name or a
  generic title.

  Japanese hand reading, while English capitalization and Chinese's
  range/area reading allow other interpretations. The referent is unknown.

  with members of King Reynor's court, but the proper-name spellings and
  court terminology need wider alignment.

  English and Chinese can refer to the army, while Japanese explicitly says
  the old official revolted. The speaker's intended subject is unresolved.

  and obtaining feathers to assemble the Wreath of Absolution are conveyed;

  his shirt as losing everything, retaining the ironic contrast with his
  former wealth.

  capitalized singular/collective `The Eldest` and Japanese's `Eldest` may

  Preordained and the promise of retribution across Mithros are retained;
  their canonical faction name has multiple Korean forms.
- ID 16827 is `not an issue`: Korean preserves both the patron's high-born
  social standing and the criticism that some status came from exploiting
  others.

  but Mithrolith, Lyenna, and the phrase's capitalization/name status need

  for them to go, and Chinese supports finding paintings with no other place
  to put them. The existing Korean invents a hidden location.
- ID 16837 remains uncertain: `마이레의 마음을 이곳에 붙잡아 두다` retains
  the metaphor, but the line alone does not establish whether it means
