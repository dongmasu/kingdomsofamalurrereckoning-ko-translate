## Decision D-18253

English ID 64074 explicitly defines the Sorrows as a force of magical
energy. ID 64093 says Bisarane needed a key to reach the Sorrows, and ID
64218 describes seeking their location. Japanese ID 65058 calls this the key
to the “power of sorrow”; Chinese likewise describes a key leading to the
Sorrows and votives that grant passage.

At ID 65058, English says the speaker must seek three more votives in
addition to the one already held so they can reach the key and gain passage.
The Japanese-derived Korean and Chinese both treat the votives as countable
physical objects. The active Korean `소로우로 가는 열쇠` preserves the
key-to-Sorrows relation in this quest context; it does not assert that the
Sorrows themselves are the House of Sorrows or mistranslate them as an
ordinary place. The separate global Korean rendering of `Sorrows` remains a
glossary question, but it does not justify retaining this row as uncertain.

Reclassify ID 65058 from uncertain to `not an issue`. Keep its current
`Texto`; do not generalize this finding to other Sorrows rows whose lore
relations differ.
