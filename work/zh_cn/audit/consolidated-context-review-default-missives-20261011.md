## Evidence and Decision

ID 64951 says that a magical object can be used to find the `Missives` again.
Repeated English quest text identifies this as the `Missives of Sable` and
describes it as an old book and as pages. The Japanese source at 64951 names
`黒紋章の書` (the Black Sigil book), and Chinese calls it `萨贝尔书卷`
(Sable's book/scroll). Related Korean entries at IDs 4084, 8283, 54854, and
55414 consistently use `세이블의 서신(Missives of Sable)`; ID 55414
explicitly calls it a book and its contents.

The active `미시브(Missives)` is an untranslated transliteration that omits
the established title identity. Change it to
`세이블의 서신(Missives of Sable)`, preserving the sentence structure and
the tracking parenthetical. No other row is changed or globally
standardized.
