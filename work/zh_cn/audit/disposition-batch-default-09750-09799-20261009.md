## Decisions

- ID 9753 is `not an issue`: Korean correctly says that only a few of the
  valuables will fetch enough gold to begin construction and preserves the
  painting, urn, box, and jewelry list.
- ID 9756 is accepted: change `베너의 시신에서` to `베너에게서`. English
  `body` here means Brenner herself/the items she carries, Japanese says to
  take the daggers from Brenner, and Chinese says not to leave them on her.
  `시신` wrongly implies she is dead.
- ID 9757 remains uncertain: English's `scavs` may be an enemy/creature name;
  Japanese transliterates it and Chinese identifies it as a creature. Korean
  `청소부들` may read as ordinary cleaners or scavengers.
- ID 9758 remains uncertain: `Mission` may name a mission house, chapel,
  monastery, or another institution; the references do not agree on its type.
- ID 9763 remains uncertain: the five-line nature poem retains its central
  images and line structure, but the antecedent of `It`, the forest-body
  metaphor, and the paradoxical ordered wilderness/natural chaos need
  integrated literary review.
- ID 9764 is `not an issue`: all references support the proper-name reading
  `Oman`; Korean's `오만(Oman)` also prevents confusion with the Korean common
  noun.
- ID 9767 is accepted: change `성전기사단이야` to `성전기사단원이야`.
  English `A Templar` and Japanese `聖堂騎士` identify Jorielle as one member,
  not the order itself.
- ID 9768 is accepted: restore the condition `If my suspicions are correct`
  and the comparison `so much more useful ... as a specimen`. The previous
  Korean invented evidence from the player's eyes and weakened the comparison.
- ID 9772 remains uncertain: the promise to pack and join the player at the
  keep shortly is preserved, but Japanese uses casual speech while Korean
  uses polite `-겠습니다`; the speaker relationship is not established here.
- ID 9777 remains uncertain: `Castellan` is capitalized and may be a formal
  office; the generic `성주` may not preserve that rank.
- ID 9778 remains uncertain: all six lines are retained, but the compressed
  images of gilded ghosts, the cuirass as life's guard, and a Fae impostor
  need integrated poetic review.
- ID 9783 remains uncertain: the broken sword, apprentice, sole repairer, and
  earlier meeting in Menetyre's heights are preserved; names and the meaning
  of `heights` need alignment.
- ID 9785 remains uncertain: the succession and expulsion prediction are
  preserved, but Tuatha's Korean spelling conflicts with other rows and
  several names have divergent forms.
- ID 9792 remains uncertain: `scuttle` is unusual in English and the
  references diverge on whether the insult means worthless people, fools, or
  something else.
- ID 9794 remains uncertain: the fortress function and three-door objective
  are preserved, but `The Fallen Hall` is a named place with divergent
  reference renderings and no established Korean form.
- ID 9795 is `not an issue`: Korean keeps the objects unspecified, preserves
  that they can be repaired but not from prison, and retains the need to manage
  without them.
- ID 9799 remains uncertain: the killing and rebuilding-plan relationship are
  retained, but `Treasures of Culn` may be a named cache rather than generic
  valuables, and the names need alignment.
