# Exceptions And Decisions

`translation-exceptions.md` is the concise index of approved terminology,
formatting, and exact-key decisions that need to remain visible during future
edits. It records each decision's scope and links to its full evidence.
`decision-register-20261009.md` is the durable source-linked register of
scoped translation findings and decisions.

An entry applies only to its stated term, IDs, variants, and context. A
historical finding is not automatically a current rule; unresolved items
remain unresolved. Project-specific decisions are not automatically rules
for another game. Dated review reports and application evidence stay in
`work/zh_cn/audit/`.
